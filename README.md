### I build things that last.

Principal Web Developer — full-stack, with a designer's eye. 16 years building for the web, the last 8 of them at [Staffbase](https://staffbase.com), where I joined as the company's first dedicated web developer and stayed through hypergrowth, acquisitions, and a full migration to a headless CMS.

I built a lot of it by hand. I also built the team, the processes, the career track, and contributed to the long-term web strategy alongside the CMO and senior stakeholders.

Full-stack in the real sense: PHP/Symfony backends, JavaScript frontends, infrastructure as code, and headless CMS work — with a designer's eye from years of building interfaces myself. I care most about systems, documentation, and processes that outlive their creator and scale beyond one person.

- 📍 Chemnitz / Leipzig area, Germany · Remote-first

### A few things I try to live by

- **Do it right, not just fast** — quality up front saves more time than corners cut.
- **Build for scale from day one** — systems designed to outlast me.
- **Think in systems, communicate the why** — most problems are symptoms of something structural.
- **Be the glue** — the unglamorous work that holds a team together.
- **From coder to orchestrator** — treat AI as a force multiplier; judgment remains irreplaceable.
